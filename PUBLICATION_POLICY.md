# Publication Policy

This repository follows the v98881 publication and release policy. The policy
applies to every change, release and published artifact of this project.

## Authorship

- The single designated human author is **v98881**.
- AI systems, language models and automated tools are never listed as authors
  or co-authors. They may assist with auditing, testing and documentation.
- No fictional contributors are added and no human contribution is invented.
- Legally required attribution of third-party components is always preserved
  (see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` where present).

## Intended use

The software is intended exclusively for personal, private, educational,
learning and other non-commercial use. Commercial deployment, business
operations, commercial services, resale, commercial redistribution and
integration into third-party commercial products are not permitted without
prior written permission. The binding terms are in `LICENSE`.

## Documentation

- All public documentation is written in English.
- Documentation must describe the project honestly: no invented features,
  capabilities, performance figures or development history.
- Documentation, screenshots and metadata must not contain unnecessary
  personal or geographical information.

## Privacy and security before publication

Before any release or publication the repository, its Git history and the
release packages are reviewed for:

- credentials, keys, tokens, certificates and other secrets,
- personal data (names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, addresses),
- private network details, logs, backups, temporary files and build artifacts,
- personal information in screenshots and documentation images,
- known security vulnerabilities and unsafe defaults.

A secret that was ever published must be revoked or rotated; deleting it from
the latest commit is not sufficient.

## Publication gate

Publication is blocked until all of the following are true:

- v98881 is the only designated human author and no AI co-author is listed;
- no credentials or unauthorized personal information are exposed;
- no critical security vulnerability and no licensing violation is open;
- ownership, purpose, non-commercial terms, disclaimer and required
  documentation are present and correct;
- basic functionality has been verified and the release contents reviewed.

Nothing is published, released or pushed to a public repository without the
explicit approval of the project owner.
